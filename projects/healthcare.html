About CarePoint

CarePoint Medical Centre is a fictional healthcare brand created as a demonstration project for BuiltByJosiah.

The website concept demonstrates how a medical facility can organise healthcare information, introduce its services and provide a simple appointment enquiry experience.

The design focuses on clarity, accessibility and a welcoming experience across different devices.
